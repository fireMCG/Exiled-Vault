# Details
- Stack Size: 1 / 10
- Limited to: 1

# Effects
- Shields: +25% of Armour also applies to Elemental Damage
- Bucklers: Gain Deflection Rating equal to 20% of Evasion Rating
- Sceptres: Companions deal 10% more Damage for each different type of dead Companion you have

# Bonded Effects

- Bucklers: 12% increased Damage for each type of Elemental Ailment on Enemy
- Sceptres: Recover 3% of maximum Life when one of your Minions is Revived
