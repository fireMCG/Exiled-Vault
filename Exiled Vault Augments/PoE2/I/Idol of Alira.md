# Details
- Stack Size: 1 / 10
- Limited to: 1
- Requires: Level 50

# Effects
- Helmets: 15% chance when you gain a Power Charge to gain an additional Power Charge
- Sceptres: If you would gain a Power Charge, Allies in your Presence gain that Charge instead

# Bonded Effects
- Helmets: +1 to Maximum Power Charges
- Sceptres: 40% increased maximum Energy Shield if you've consumed a Power Charge Recently
