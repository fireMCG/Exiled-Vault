# Details
- Limited to: 1 Ancient Augment
- Requires: Level 60

# Effects
- Body Armours: 15% of Chaos Damage from Hits taken as a Damage of a random Element
- Gloves: Gain 1% of Damage as Extra Damage of a random Element per / Rune Socketed in Equipped Items
- Boots: 50% increased Runic Ward Regeneration Rate while Sprinting

# Bonded Effects
- Body Armours: +13% to Chaos Resistance
- Gloves: 20% increased Elemental Damage
- Boots: 15% increased Runic Ward Cost Efficiency
