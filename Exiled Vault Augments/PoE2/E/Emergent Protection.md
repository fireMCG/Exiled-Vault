# Details
- Limited to: 1 Ancient Augment
- Requires: Level 60

# Effects
- Helmets: Gain 1 Endurance Charge on reaching Low Life, only once every 2 seconds
- Gloves: Banners also grant 2% of Life Regenerated per second to affected targets
- Boots: When you stop Sprinting, gain Guard equal to 4% of maximum Life per second spent Sprinting, up to a maximum of 20%, for 4 seconds

# Bonded Effects
- Helmets: 40% increased Endurance Charge Duration
- Gloves: Regenerate 0.5% of maximum Life per second
- Boots: 20% increased Guard gained
