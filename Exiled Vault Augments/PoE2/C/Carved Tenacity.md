# Details
- Limited to: 1 Ancient Augment
- Requires: Level 60

# Effects
- Helmets: Enemies have no Critical Damage Bonus for 4 seconds after you Blind them
- Gloves: Enemies you Critically Hit get 100% reduced Life Regeneration Rate for 4 seconds
- Boots: Your speed is Unaffected by Slows while Sprinting

# Bonded Effects
- Helmets: 20% increased Blind Effect
- Gloves: 15% increased Critical Hit Chance
- Boots: 8% increased Movement Speed while Sprinting
