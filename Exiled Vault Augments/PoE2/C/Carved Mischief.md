# Details
- Limited to: 1 Ancient Augment
- Requires: Level 60

# Effects
- Helmets: Gain Guard equal to 10% of maximum Life for 4 seconds on taking Savage Hit
- Gloves: +5% to maximum Block chance if you've Blocked with a raised Shield Recently
- Body Armours: 200% increased Stun Threshold if you've been Stunned Recently

# Bonded Effects
- Helmets: Buffs on you expire 10% slower
- Gloves: 20% increased Block chance
- Body Armours: 25% increased Stun Threshold
