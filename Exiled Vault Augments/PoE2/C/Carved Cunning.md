# Details
- Limited to: 1 Ancient Augment
- Requires: Level 60

# Effects
- Helmets: Enemies which are on Full Life cannot Evade your Hits
- Body Armours: Prevent +5% of Damage from Deflected Hits if you've / Deflected no Hits Recently
- Boots: Gain Onslaught for 4 seconds when your Marks Activate

# Bonded Effects
- Helmets: 30% increased Accuracy Rating
- Body Armours: 8% increased Deflection Rating
- Boots: Buffs on you expire 10% slower
