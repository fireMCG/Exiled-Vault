#### Pirate's Journal
Lussi is no mother of Brine. Her vision for our future is small. These new Brinerot are feeble, inbred, and stupid. They shrink in fear at my glorious visage. Fools! My form is a gift from {Tsoatha} itself! Rise {Kishara}, the true mother, whose children founded our clan. You dominated the seas, so that every shore knew our name. With you at our helm, we will sail to the ends of the world, and take every power it has to offer! Velka
