# Gwendolyn Albright on Monsters
Gwendolyn Albright: The monsters have always been tough out here. That's not new. What is new is how they're behaving. Used to be you'd only find trouble if you walked into a den, or trampled a hive. You'd have to make a mistake to provoke an attack. Something changed. Now... it's like they're hunting us. Nowhere in the jungle is safe. Maybe we've overstayed our welcome.
