# Gwendolyn Albright on Other Camps
Gwendolyn Albright: We've got two more camps, close to the Vaal ruins we're exploring. We really only return to the Encampment for supplies or more hands to hold treasure. Sebastian leads the camp in the Infested Barrens. He's been with this expedition longer than I have. He's one of the few veterans left. A newcomer, Yorick, leads the camp in the Chimeral Wetlands. Oswald was hesitant but... Yorick was insistent. Hard to deny a volunteer, with so few of us left. Yorick's no adventurer, but he must be an archaeologist or something, with how he goes on about Vaal architecture and history. It's hard to get him to stop. I worry for Yorick. The monsters are getting more dangerous. And some of the newcomers joining our expedition have been... untrustworthy.
