# Sebastian Carroway on State of Things
Sebastian Carroway: I've witnessed unspeakable things out here. Man I'd worked with for years, on multiple expeditions, died after eating some bad berries. 'How bad,' you say? Melted his face off. And that didn't even kill him. He was possessed. Had to cut off his head and burn it for him to stop screaming. And it's been getting worse. Starting to think the Vaal cursed some treasure, and the jungle is enacting their vengeance on us. Anyway. Thanks for saving the camp. One more day I avoid ending up in Oswald's death journal.
