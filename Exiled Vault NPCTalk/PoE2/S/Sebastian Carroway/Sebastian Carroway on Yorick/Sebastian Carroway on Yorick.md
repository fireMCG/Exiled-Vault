# Sebastian Carroway on Yorick
Sebastian Carroway: Yorick's one of many newcomers who should have never been hired. He's a starry-eyed fool, and a liability. He's downright obsessed with the Vaal. Told me he doesn't even need the money, just wants to 'see their legacy with his own eyes.' 'You'll be lucky to leave here with eyes,' I said. I asked him to check in every week or so. It's been a month... Knowing him, he's either stuck in a book, or stuck in a trap with a book.
