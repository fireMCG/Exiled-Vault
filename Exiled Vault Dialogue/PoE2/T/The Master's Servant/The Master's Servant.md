#### Where We Are From
You... ask us such a question? You care to know? We are not worthy of such compassion! My brother and I... we were bound for Pondium to serve a master with quite a reputation... But it seems fate intervened. The ship sank and we... we floated our way across the sea in a barrel. A couple of our brothers, well... they didn't make it. But they meant our survival, at least. Once we landed ashore... we were masterless. A fate worse than death! We fought each other bloody out of sheer anger and confusion. Then, you appeared... a light in the darkness! You saved us, master. And so... we are... {eternally} grateful.
